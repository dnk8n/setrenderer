# Code of conduct

No dress code, no gender code, just bass.

setrender's videos are full of people of every identity dancing together, and this project wants the same of its community. Everyone is welcome here regardless of age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, level of experience, nationality, appearance, religion or musical taste.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give and take feedback gracefully, credit others' work, and focus on what is best for the project and the people in it.
- **Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone, make sexualised comments or advances, publish someone's private information, or keep going after someone has asked you to stop.

It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, tell the maintainer, [@dnk8n](https://github.com/dnk8n). If it can't be raised in public, open an issue titled "Conduct: please contact me" with no details, and the maintainer will get in touch privately. Reports are handled promptly and in confidence. The maintainer may remove comments, edit or close contributions, or block people who break this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
